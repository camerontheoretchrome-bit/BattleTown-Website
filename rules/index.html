Server Rules

BattleTown is meant to be fun, fair and safe. By playing, you agree to these rules. Staff decide what counts as breaking them, and the examples below are not a complete list.

1. RESPECT

No abusive, hateful or sexual language. No harassing, threatening or bullying anyone. This covers chat, private messages, voice chat, names, signs, books and Maps. Never threaten to dox, DDoS or hack anyone, and never share anyone's personal information.

2. FAIR PLAY

No hacked clients, macros, auto-clickers, scripts or any mod that gives you an advantage. Allowed: performance mods (Sodium, Lithium, Iris), Simple Voice Chat, minimaps without radar or cave view, and the Lunar, Feather and LabyMod clients. Not sure? Ask staff before you use it.

3. EXPLOITS AND BUGS

Don't use bugs, glitches or unintended mechanics to get ahead. Report them with /report, then A BUG.

4. MATCHES

No teaming in Free-for-All or Last Alive. No win-trading, throwing or arranging results in any game mode. No AFK play, idling or farming Money, XP or stats, including against dummies. No sabotaging your own team.

5. CONTENT

Names, skins, signs, books, item names and Creative Maps must be fine for all ages. No sexual, hateful or graphic content, and no real-world hate symbols.

6. TRADING AND ADVERTISING

Don't buy, sell, trade or share accounts. Don't trade Money, cosmetics or anything else from BattleTown for real money or outside goods. No gambling. Don't advertise other servers, websites or social media.

7. YOUR ACCOUNT

You're responsible for everything done on your account. Don't share it, and don't use another account to dodge a punishment.

8. STAFF

Don't pretend to be staff, and follow staff instructions. To report a player, use /report.

PUNISHMENTS

Depending on what happened and your history: a warning, a mute (text and voice), a kick, or a ban for 1, 7 or 30 days or forever. Staff may also remove Money, items, stats, levels or Maps gained by breaking the rules. Repeat offences are punished harder.

To appeal: email battletowntm@gmail.com or open a ticket on the BattleTown Discord: discord.gg/TJ5wBJYCc

Privacy Policy

This explains what BattleTown keeps about you, why, and what you can do about it.

WHAT WE KEEP

Your Minecraft name and UUID, to know who you are and save your progress.

Your IP address, when you join or download the resource pack, for security, bans and stopping attacks.

Your chat messages and commands, kept in server logs, for moderation and reports.

WHAT WE KEEP (CONTINUED)

What you build on Maps, so Maps can be reviewed and shared.

Your gameplay: matches, kills, stats, playtime, Money, cosmetics and levels, to run the game, leaderboards and balance.

Reports and bug reports from you or about you, for moderation and fixing bugs.

Anti-cheat signals (GrimAC), to catch cheating.

Purchase records from Tebex: what you bought, when, and the transaction ID, to deliver it and handle disputes.

VOICE CHAT

BattleTown uses Simple Voice Chat. Voice goes live to nearby players and is not recorded or stored by us.

WHAT WE DON'T KEEP

We never see your card details: Tebex handles payment. We don't ask for your real name, email or location in game.

WHO WE SHARE IT WITH

Tebex for payments, Bloom.host, which runs the server in the United States, and the police if the law requires it. We don't sell your data.

HOW LONG WE KEEP IT

Server and chat logs: 90 days. Account and game data: while your account exists. Punishment records: as long as needed to enforce bans.

CHILDREN

BattleTown is for players aged 13 and up. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has played, contact us and we'll delete their data.

YOUR RIGHTS

You can ask to see or delete your data at battletowntm@gmail.com. We may keep some records, like bans and purchases, where safety or the law needs them. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights.

CHANGES

We'll update this policy in game and ask you to agree again.

THIS WEBSITE

battletown.store is a plain website hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and has no ads or trackers. GitHub may log your IP address when you visit, to run and protect its service.

The home page asks mcsrvstat.us, a public server-status service, whether the BattleTown server is online, so your browser contacts that service when you visit it.

The Store page talks to Tebex to show what's for sale and to keep your basket. It sends Tebex the Minecraft name you type there, and your browser remembers that name and your basket on your own device. Payment happens on Tebex's checkout, under Tebex's own terms and privacy policy.

If you turn on the music, your browser remembers that for the rest of your visit. Music never plays until you tap it.
