Creative

Build your own Map

Buy a Map at the Creative Shop in the Hub and build your own arena in Creative. If BattleTown approves it, it joins public matches.

  1. Buy a Map with in-game BattleBucks. Pick a size.
  2. Build your arena in Creative, with the build tools.
  3. Set Battle Spawns so a full match can play on it, then test it.
  4. Send it to BattleTown. A curator plays it and decides.
  5. Get approved and it becomes a Community Map in public Map votes, with your name on it.
  6. Earn royalties: $1,000 BattleBucks every time a public match on your Map finishes.
