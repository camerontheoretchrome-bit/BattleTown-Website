Game modes

Pick your fight

Step onto a Battle Plaza platform in the Hub, or type /play. Quick Play puts you in whichever mode fills first. Players vote on the Map, then the fight starts.

1v1

Duel

  • Best of 3 rounds. Win 2 rounds to win the Duel.
  • Each round lasts up to 90 seconds.
  • If time runs out, the player with more health left wins the round.

3–8

Free For All

  • Everyone for themselves. You respawn after 5 seconds.
  • First to 10 kills wins, or most kills after 6 minutes.
  • No teaming.

2v2 · 4v4

Team Deathmatch

  • Blue team against Red team. You respawn after 5 seconds.
  • First team to 10 kills (2v2) or 20 kills (4v4) wins.
  • Bring your friends: party up and queue together.

4 · 8

Last Alive

  • One life each. The last fighter standing wins.
  • Out of the fight? Watch the rest of the match as a spectator.
  • No teaming.

Every finished public match pays BattleBucks and rank XP. Win, get kills and assists, and spend it on cosmetics or your own Map.
