Game modes

Pick your fight

Matches last 4 to 7 minutes. Vote on the Map, count down, and go.

1v1

Duel

  • Best of 3 rounds. Win 2 rounds to win the Duel.

3–8

Free For All

  • Everyone for themselves. You respawn after 5 seconds.

2v2 · 4v4

Team Deathmatch

  • Blue team against Red team. You respawn after 5 seconds.

4 · 8

Last Alive

  • One life each. The last fighter standing wins.

Creative Maps

Build the arena. Get paid.

Buy a Map, build your own arena in Creative and send it to BattleTown. If it's approved, it joins public matches with your name on it, and you earn $1,000 BattleBucks every time a match on it finishes.
