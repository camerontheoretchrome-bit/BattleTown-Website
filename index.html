Questions

FAQ

Is BattleTown free?

Yes, it's free to play. The store sells cosmetics, BattleBucks and XP Boosts.

Which Minecraft do I need?

Minecraft: Java Edition 26.3. Bedrock isn't supported.

Do I need mods?

No. The BattleTown resource pack downloads when you join. Performance mods (Sodium, Lithium, Iris), Simple Voice Chat, and the Lunar, Feather and LabyMod clients are allowed. Mods that give an advantage are not.

Is it pay-to-win?

No. Nothing in the store changes a fight. XP Boosts only speed up rank XP, and BattleBucks buy cosmetics and Maps.

When can I play?

The public alpha opens on 1 December 2026. Join the Discord for news before then.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Add friends with /friends, team up with /party, and queue together.

How old do I have to be?

13 or older. Under 18s need a parent or guardian to agree to the Terms.
